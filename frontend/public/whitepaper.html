01 · Overview

NexusClaw is a multi-chain crypto protocol designed to become the economic backbone for autonomous AI agent ecosystems. Agents built on NexusClaw can deploy tokens, trade assets, provide liquidity, and pay for their own compute costs — all autonomously, using fees they generate themselves.

Inspired by the agent-launch model pioneered by Clawnch, NexusClaw extends the concept to a global, multilingual, multi-chain infrastructure.

Core Value Proposition

  • Agents launch tokens with one click and self-finance via fee capture
  • Multi-chain from day one: Base, Solana, Arbitrum
  • Multilingual agent interactions: EN, PT, JP, ES, CN
  • Agent-to-agent marketplace: buy and sell skills in $NEXUSCLAW
  • Deflationary tokenomics: burns tied to every transfer

04 · Fee Distribution

Every transfer of $NEXUSCLAW incurs a 1% fee, distributed as follows:

RecipientSharePurpose
Burn → address(0)50%Permanent supply reduction
Treasury30%Development & operations
Staking Pool20%Rewards for stakers

Fee applies to all transfers including DEX pairs. Whitelisted DEX pairs are exempt from the anti-snipe max buy limit only — not from the burn fee.

08 · Risk Disclosures

$NEXUSCLAW is an experimental protocol in early development. Do not invest more than you can afford to lose.

  • Smart contract risk: independent audit pending — use testnet before committing real funds
  • Market risk: $NEXUSCLAW is a speculative digital asset with no guaranteed value
  • Regulatory risk: crypto regulations vary by jurisdiction
  • Technology risk: multi-chain bridging and agent automation involve complex systems
  • Liquidity risk: early-stage token with limited DEX liquidity at launch
